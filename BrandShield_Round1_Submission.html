CodeNova India 2026 Inkloom AI Challenge Round 1: The Plan

BrandShield AI — Intelligent Brand Governance Platform

An enterprise-grade, agentic pre-publication brand governance system featuring evidence-grounded verification, intent-preserving fixes, human oversight, and the Brand Guard MCP Server.

Executive Summary & The Winning Thesis

Most teams attempting this challenge will build: "Paste text into a box → an LLM prompt lists generic issues." That is merely a chatbot with a checklist. It hallucinates rules, breaks creator intent, creates reviewer fatigue, and offers zero enterprise accountability.

BrandShield AI is engineered around five core properties:

  • 1. Evidence-First Findings: "No citation, no flag." Every issue must cite the exact guideline clause, approved fact, or asset version. Uncited findings are killed by an automated Verifier agent.
  • 2. Hybrid Detection: Deterministic checks (terminology, color contrast math, readability, alt-text) run alongside LLM semantic evaluations for reproducible, explainable outcomes.
  • 3. Intent-Preserving Fixes: AI never blindly rewrites content. An Intent Guard agent validates that suggested fixes preserve original creator intent and introduce no unsubstantiated claims.
  • 4. Human Control by Design: Role-based access, risk-calibrated approval routing, mandatory override rationales, and an immutable audit log. No auto-publishing.
  • 5. Guideline Health Intelligence: Aggregates human overrides and repeat mistakes to detect ambiguous or outdated rules, allowing an AI Clarifier to draft updated guidelines for administrator approval.
  • 6. Signature Differentiator (MCP Server & Pre-Flight API): Exposes brand rules and pre-flight checks via an MCP (Model Context Protocol) Server, allowing external AI assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Cursor) and agency tools to check brand rules before drafting content.

1. Understanding of the Problem Statement (25 Points)

1.1 Problem Definition & Context

In fast-scaling companies, content production is decentralized across internal employees, external agencies, and generative AI tools. Marketing, sales, support, and product teams simultaneously publish across LinkedIn, newsletters, blogs, documentation, ad creatives, and customer emails. Without automated governance, organizations face severe brand dilution, factual errors, outdated pricing escapes, and PR liabilities.

1.2 Industry Evidence & Market Reality

  • Economic Cost of Inconsistency: According to the Lucidpress / Marq State of Brand Consistency Report, consistent brand presentation increases company revenue by 10% to 23%, while 68% of organizations report that inconsistent marketing creates customer confusion and lost deals.
  • The Guideline Gap: While 85%+ of companies maintain detailed brand style guides, fewer than 30% of employees regularly consult them. Static 40-page PDFs sitting in Google Drive fail to influence daily drafting workflows.
  • Review Latency Bottlenecks: Manual review loops take an average of 2 to 4 business days per asset, leading creators to bypass formal approvals during fast-moving social campaigns.

1.3 Five Verifiable Real-World Brand Failures (Case Studies)

Case Study What Failed Root Cause & Business Consequence
Google Gemini / Search Overviews (2024) AI generated ungrounded and nonsensical factual claims in public demos. Lack of an automated claims-verification and factual-grounding layer against approved source databases. Resulted in severe brand equity loss and PR backlash.
Burger King UK (IWD Campaign) Standalone tweet: "Women belong in the kitchen" posted without upfront context. Lack of contextual sensitivity and messaging-risk pre-flight checks before publishing. Resulted in global social media outrage.
Adidas Boston Marathon (2017) Marketing subject line: "Congrats, you survived the Boston Marathon!" Absence of negative-terminology filters and historical-event sensitivity checks in marketing automation pipelines.
KFC Germany (2022) Automated notification promoting fried chicken on Kristallnacht. Automated promotional triggers with zero calendar, holiday sensitivity, or brand compliance governance. Led to an international public apology.
Sports Illustrated / Gannett (2023–24) AI-written articles published with fabricated author personas and factual errors. Publishing synthetic copy without strict provenance, factual verification, and brand voice validation. Severe credibility loss and union protests.

Hardest Technical Challenge & Solution: LLM Hallucination vs. Evidence Grounding

The Challenge: Eliminating False Positives in Governance

If an automated system invents nonexistent brand guidelines or misinterprets casual phrases as violations, content creators immediately lose trust and bypass the system. Standard monolithic LLM prompts regularly hallucinate rules.

Our Architectural Solution: The Verifier / Critic Gate

We enforce a strict Pydantic contract where every finding must return a verifiable source_id, version_hash, and exact quote from the active Brand Pack. The Verifier agent cross-checks every candidate flag against the database. If the citation does not exist, or if the quote fails semantic alignment, the flag is silently dropped before reaching the user. "No citation, no flag."
